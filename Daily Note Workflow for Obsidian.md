
At the beginning of each day, start by creating a **Daily Note**. This note will serve as a central log for the day's activities and reflections.

## Structure of the Daily Notes

1. **What I Want to Do** - Aspirations or goals for the day.
2. **What I Need to Do** - Essential or scheduled tasks that must be completed.
3. **What I'm Going to Do** - A realistic plan or commitment based on the above.
## Learning and Knowledge Capture

When you learn something new - whether through reading books, watching videos or other means - **record it in the Daily Note first**. Then, create links from that entry to the relevant permanent notes, topic pages, or knowledge hubs within your Obsidian vault.
