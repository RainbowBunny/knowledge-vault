## Definition

> [!definition] Category $\mathsf{Grp}$
> [[Category]] $\mathsf{Grp}$ consists of
> - $\mathsf{Obj}(\mathsf{Grp})$ is the class of all [[Group]].
> - For $G, H$ groups, we define
> $$\mathsf{Hom}_\mathsf{Grp}(G, H)$$
> to be the set of [[Group Homomorphism]] $G \rightarrow H$.

## Property

### Universal Property

> [!proposition] Initial and Final Object
> [[Trivial Group]] are both [[Universal Property#Initial Objects|Initial]] and [[Universal Property#Final Objects|Final]] in $\mathsf{Grp}$.

### Product

> [!proposition]
> With operation defined component-wise, $G \times H$ is a [[Universal Property#Products|Product]] in $\mathsf{Grp}$.
