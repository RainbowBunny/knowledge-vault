## Definition

> [!definition] Groupoid
> Extends:: [[Category]].
>
> ---
> A [[Category]] in which every morphism is an [[Morphism#Isomorphism|isomorphism]] is called a **groupoid**. `[Rie Def 1.1.11]`

## Property

> [!proposition] Maximal groupoid — every category contains one, on all of its objects
> Any category $\mathsf C$ contains a maximal groupoid: the subcategory containing all of the objects of $\mathsf C$ and only those morphisms that are isomorphisms. `[Rie Lemma 1.1.13]`

## Example

> [!example] A group is a one-object groupoid
> A [[Group]] is essentially a category with one object in which every morphism is an isomorphism: the morphisms are the elements of the group and composition is the group operation. `[Lei Ex 1.1.8(c)]`
