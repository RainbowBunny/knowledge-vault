## Definition

> [!definition] Category
> A **category** $\mathsf{C}$ consists of:
> - A collection of Object $\mathsf{Obj}(\mathsf{C})$.
> - A collection of [[Morphism]] (arrow) $\mathsf{Hom}_{\mathsf{C}}(A, B)$ for each pair of objects $A, B$.
> - An [[Associativity|Associative]], [[Identity Element]] binary operation $\circ$ between Morphism called [[Function#Composition|Composition]].
> - The sets $\mathsf{Hom}_{\mathsf{C}}(A, B)$ and $\mathsf{Hom}_{\mathsf{C}}(C, D)$ be *disjoint* unless $A = C, B = D$.

> [!remark]
> $f \in \mathsf{Hom}_{\mathcal{C}}(A, B)$ is the general form of $f: A \rightarrow B$.
