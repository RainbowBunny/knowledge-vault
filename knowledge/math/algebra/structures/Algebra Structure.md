## Definition

> [!definition] Parameters
> - $A$: a real vector space ([[Vector Spaces]]).
> - $m: A \times A \to A$, $(a, b) \mapsto ab$: a bilinear map ([[Bilinearity]]), the multiplication.
> - $1 \in A$: a nonzero element, the unit.

> [!definition] Algebra
> Extends:: [[Vector Spaces]].
>
> ---
> An **algebra** is a real vector space $A$ equipped with a bilinear multiplication $m$ and a nonzero unit $1$ such that, for all $a \in A$,
> - $1a = a$,
> - $a1 = a$.
>
> It is **associative** if $m$ satisfies [[Associativity]], and **alternative** if the subalgebra generated by any two elements is associative ([[Alternativity]]). `[Bae02 §1.1]`

> [!definition] Parameters
> - $A$: an algebra.

> [!definition] Division Algebra
> An algebra $A$ is a **division algebra** if, for $a, b \in A$, $ab = 0$ implies $a = 0$ or $b = 0$. `[Bae02 §1.1]`

## Property

### Frobenius theorem

> [!theorem] Frobenius theorem — a finite-dimensional associative real division algebra is $\mathbb R$, $\mathbb C$ or $\mathbb H$
> Finite-dimensional associative division algebras over the real numbers are each isomorphic to one of:
> - $\textbf{R}$ (the real numbers)
> - $\textbf{C}$ (the complex numbers)
> - $\textbf{H}$ (the quaternions)
>
> These algebras have real dimension 1, 2, and 4, respectively. `[BrSh19 Thm 2.1]`

## Example

### Quaternions

This is the extensions of the complex number system where each element $x$ is a 4-dimensions vector:
$$x = a + b i + c j + d k$$
where the basis vector $1, i, j, k$ has the multiplication table:

| x   | 1   | i   | j   | k   |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1   | 1   | i   | j   | k   |
| i   | i   | -1  | k   | -j  |
| j   | j   | -k  | -1  | i   |
| k   | k   | j   | -i  | -1  |

Which follows the rule: $i^2 = j^2 = k^2 = i j k = -1$ `[Bae02 §1]`

Note: Multiplication is not commutative but associative.

### Octonion

Octonion is the extensions of quaternions system by combining complex number system and quaternions number system:

$$x = (a, b) = a + \omega b$$
Where $\omega^2 = -1$ and $a, b$ are elements in quaternions. Multiplication of pairs is
$$(a, b)(c, d) = (ac - db^*, a^*d + cb),$$
where $^*$ is quaternion conjugation. `[Bae02 §2.2]`

Note: Multiplication is not commutative and not associative, however, octonion is alternative. `[Bae02 §1.1]`
