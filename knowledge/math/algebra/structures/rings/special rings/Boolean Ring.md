## Definition

> [!definition] Boolean Ring
> Extends: [[Ring]] $(R, +, *)$
> - $*$ is [[Idempotence]] on $R$.

## Property

> [!theorem] Characteristic of Boolean Ring
> Every nonzero Boolean ring is a [[Commutative Ring]] with characteristic $2$.
