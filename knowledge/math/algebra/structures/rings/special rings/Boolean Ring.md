## Definition

> [!definition] Boolean Ring
> Extends:: [[Ring]] $(R, +, *)$
> - $*$ is [[Idempotence]] on $R$.

## Property

> [!theorem] Boolean Ring is Commutative Ring
> Every nonzero Boolean ring is a [[Commutative Ring]].

> [!theorem] Characteristic of Boolean Ring
> Also, Every nonzero Boolean ring has characteristic $2$.

> [!corollary]
> A Boolean [[Integral Domain]] has no zero divisors, so $R = \{0, 1\}$; nonzero gives $0 \neq 1$, hence $R \cong \mathbb{Z} / 2 \mathbb{Z}$.
