## Integer Subgroup

> [!proposition]
> Let $G \subseteq \mathbb Z$ be a subgroup. Then $G = d \mathbb Z$ for some $d \geq 0$.

> [!proposition]
> Let $n > 0$ be an integer and let $G \subseteq \mathbb Z / n \mathbb Z$ be a subgroup. Then $G$ is the cyclic subgroup of $\mathbb Z / n \mathbb Z$ generated by $[d]_n$, for some divisor $d$ of $n$.
