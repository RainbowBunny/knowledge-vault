## Definition

> [!definition] Ideal
> Let $(R, +, \cdot)$ be a [[Ring]]. A [[Subgroup]] $I$ of $(R, +)$ is
> - Left-ideal of $R$ ($rI \subseteq I$ for all $r \in R$):
> $$(\forall r \in R)(\forall a \in I): ra \in I$$
> - Right-ideal of $R$ ($Ir \subseteq I$ for all $r \in R$):
> $$(\forall r \in R)(\forall a \in I): ar \in I$$
> - Two-sided ideal if both.
