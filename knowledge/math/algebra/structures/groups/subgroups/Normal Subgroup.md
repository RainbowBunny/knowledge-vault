## Definition

> [!definition] Normal Subgroups
> A subgroup $N$ of a group $G$ is normal if $\forall g \in G, \forall n \in N$,
> $$gng^{-1} \in N$$

## Property

> [!lemma] 
> If $\varphi: G \rightarrow G'$ is any group homomorphism, then $\ker  \varphi$ is normal subgroup of $G$.
