## Basic Definition

> [!definition] Monoid
> A **monoid** is a set $\{g_1, g_2 \dots\}$ together with a binary operation $\circ$ that satisfies [[Closure]], [[Associativity]], [[Identity Element]].

> [!definition] Monoid (Alternative)
> Alternatively, a **monoid** is a [[Semigroup]] that the binary operation $\circ$ that satisfies [[Identity Element]].
