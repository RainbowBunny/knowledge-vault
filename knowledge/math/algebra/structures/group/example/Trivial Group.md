## Definition

> [!definition] Parameters
> - $G = \{e\}$: a singleton [[Set]].
> - $\star$: the only [[Binary Operation]] on $G$.

> [!definition] Trivial Group
> Instantiates:: [[Group]].
>
> ---
> Let $G = \{e\}$ be a singleton and $\star$ is the operation defined as
> $$e \star e = e.$$
> The group $(G, \star)$ is usually called the **trivial group**.
>
> `[Alu II.1.2]`

## Property

The trivial group is both initial and final in [[Category Group|Grp]] and in [[Category Abelian|Ab]]; see those notes.
