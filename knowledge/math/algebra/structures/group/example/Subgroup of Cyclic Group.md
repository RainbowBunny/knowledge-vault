## Definition

> [!theorem] Subgroup of Cyclic Group — every subgroup of a cyclic group is cyclic
> ### Scope
> A [[Cyclic Group]] $G$ and a [[Subgroup]] $H \leq G$.
>
> ---
> ### Property
> [[Subgroup]] of [[Cyclic Group]] is itself cyclic group: $H$ is cyclic. `[Jud Thm 4.10]`

## Property

> [!proposition] Subgroups of $\mathbb Z$ — each is $d\mathbb Z$
> Let $G \subseteq \mathbb{Z}$ be a [[Subgroup]]. Then $G = d\mathbb{Z}$ for some $d \geq 0$. `[Jud Cor 4.11]`

> [!proposition] Subgroups of $\mathbb Z / n\mathbb Z$ — each is generated by the class of a divisor of $n$
> Let $n > 0$ be an integer and let $G \subseteq \mathbb{Z} / n \mathbb{Z}$ be a [[Subgroup]]. Then $G$ is the cyclic subgroup of $\mathbb{Z}/n\mathbb{Z}$ generated by $[d]_n$, for some divisor $d$ of $n$. `[Jud Thm 4.10, Thm 4.13]`
