## Definition

> [!definition] Parameters
> - [[Group]]: Import $G$.
> - $A$: a [[Set]] (the generators).
> - $F(A)$: the [[Free Group]] on $A$.
> - $R$: the normal subgroup of $F(A)$ generated by the relations.

> [!definition] Group Presentation
> Requires:: [[Free Group]].
>
> ---
> A **presentation** of a group $G$ is an explicit [[Group Isomorphism|Isomorphism]]
> $$G \cong \frac{F(A)}{R}$$
> where $A$ is a set, $F(A)$ is the [[Free Group]] and $R$ is the [[Normal Subgroup]] of $F(A)$ generated by a set of 'relations'; one writes $(A \mid R)$. `[Alu II.8.2]` `[Gar21 Def I.9.4]`
