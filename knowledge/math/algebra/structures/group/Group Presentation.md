## Definition

> [!definition] Group Presentation
> A **presentation** of a group $G$ is an explicit [[Homomorphism|Isomorphism]]
> $$G \cong \frac{F(A)}{R}$$
> where $A$ is a set, $F(A)$ is the [[Free Group]] and $R$ is a [[Subgroup]] of 'relations'.
