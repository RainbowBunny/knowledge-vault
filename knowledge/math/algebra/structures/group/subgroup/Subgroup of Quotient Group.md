## Definition

> [!proposition]
> Let $H$ be a [[Normal Subgroup]] of a [[Group]] $G$. Then for every [[Subgroup]] $K$ of $G$ containing $H, K/H$ may be identified with a subgroup of $G/H$. The function $u(K) = K/H$ is a [[Bijection]] preserving inclusion.

> [!proposition]
> Let $H$ be a [[Normal Subgroup]] of a [[Group]] $G$, and let $N$ be a subgroup of $G$ containing $H$. Then $N/H$ is normal in $G/H$ if and only if $N$ is normal in $G$, and in this case
> $$\frac{G/H}{N/H} \cong \frac{G}{N}$$
