## Definition

> [!theorem] Correspondence theorem — the subgroups of $G/H$ are the $K/H$ with $H \leq K \leq G$
> ### Scope
> A [[Group]] $G$ and a [[Normal Subgroup]] $H$ of $G$.
>
> ---
> ### Property
> For every [[Subgroup]] $K$ of $G$ containing $H$, $K/H$ may be identified with a subgroup of $G/H$. The function $u(K) = K/H$ is a [[Bijection]] preserving inclusion, from the subgroups of $G$ containing $H$ to the subgroups of $G/H$.
>
> `[Alu II §8.3]` `[Jud Thm 11.2.4]`

## Property

> [!theorem] Third isomorphism theorem — $(G/H)/(N/H) \cong G/N$
> Let $H$ be a [[Normal Subgroup]] of a [[Group]] $G$, and let $N$ be a subgroup of $G$ containing $H$. Then $N/H$ is normal in $G/H$ if and only if $N$ is normal in $G$, and in this case
> $$\frac{G/H}{N/H} \cong \frac{G}{N}$$
> `[Alu II §8.3]` `[Jud Thm 11.2.4]` `[Jud Thm 11.2.5]`
