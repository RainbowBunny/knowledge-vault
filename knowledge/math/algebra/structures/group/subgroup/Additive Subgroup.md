## Definition

> [!definition] Parameters
> - $m$: a positive integer.
> - $L \subseteq \mathbb R^m$: a subset.

> [!definition] Additive Subgroup
> Extends:: [[Subgroup]].
>
> ---
> A subset $L$ of $\mathbb R^m$ is an **additive subgroup** if it contains $\mathbf 0$ and is closed under addition and subtraction, i.e. $L$ is a [[Subgroup]] of $(\mathbb R^m, +)$. `[Pei16 §2.2.1]`
