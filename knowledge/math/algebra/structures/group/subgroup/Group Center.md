## Definition

> [!definition] Group Center
> Specialize:: [[Center]].
> 
> ---
> The **center** of $G$, denote $Z(G)$, is the [[Subgroup]] $\mathrm{ker} \; \sigma$ of $G$.
> Concretely, the **center** of $G$ is
> $$Z(G) = \{g \in G \mid (\forall a \in G): ag = ga\}$$
