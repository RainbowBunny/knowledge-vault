## Definition

> [!definition] Parameters
> - $G, H$: [[Abelian Group|abelian groups]].

> [!definition] Category $\mathsf{Ab}$
> Instantiates:: [[Category]].
>
> ---
> [[Category]] $\mathsf{Ab}$ consists of
> - $\mathsf{Obj}(\mathsf{Ab})$ is the class of all [[Abelian Group]].
> - For $G, H$ abelian groups, we define
> $$\mathsf{Hom}_\mathsf{Ab}(G, H)$$
> to be the set of [[Group Homomorphism]] $G \rightarrow H$.
>
> `[Alu II.3.2]` `[Lei §1.2]`

## Property

### Universal Property

> [!proposition] Initial and Final Object — the trivial group is both
> [[Trivial Group]] are both [[Universal Property|Initial]] and [[Universal Property|Final]] in $\mathsf{Ab}$. `[Alu II.3]`
