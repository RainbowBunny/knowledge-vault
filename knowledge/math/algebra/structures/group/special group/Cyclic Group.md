## Definition

> [!definition] Parameters
> - $G$: a [[Group]].
> - $n$: a positive integer.

> [!definition] Cyclic Group
> Extends:: [[Abelian Group]].
> Requires:: [[Modular Arithmetic]].
>
> ---
> A group $G$ is **cyclic** if it is [[Group Isomorphism|isomorphic]] to $\mathbb Z$ or to $C_n = \mathbb Z / n \mathbb Z$ for some $n$. `[Alu II §4.3]`

> [!remark] Generator form
> Equivalently, $G$ is cyclic if $G = \langle a \rangle = \{a^k : k \in \mathbb Z\}$ for some $a \in G$, called a **generator** of $G$ `[Jud §4.1]`: a cyclic group of infinite order is isomorphic to $\mathbb Z$ `[Jud Thm 9.1.7]`, and a cyclic group of order $n$ to $\mathbb Z_n$ `[Jud Thm 9.1.8]`. Every cyclic group is abelian `[Jud Thm 4.1.9]`, hence the parent [[Abelian Group]].

## Property

> [!proposition] Presentation of $C_n$ — one generator $x$ with the relation $x^n = e$
> $C_n$ is generated by one element $x$, with the relation $x^n = e$. `[Alu II §4.3]`
