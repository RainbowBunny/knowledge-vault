## Definition

> [!definition] Cyclic Group
> Requires:: [[Modular Arithmetic]]
> 
> ---
> A group $G$ is **cyclic** if it is [[Group Homomorphism|Isomorphism]] to $\mathbb Z$ or to $C_n = \mathbb Z / n \mathbb Z$ for some $n$.

## Property

> [!proposition]
> $C_n$ is generated by one element $x$, with the relation $x^n = e$.
