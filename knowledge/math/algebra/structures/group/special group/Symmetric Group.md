## Definition

> [!definition] Symmetric Group
> Let $A$ be a set. The **symmetric group**, or **group of permutations** of $A$, denoted $S_A$, is the group $\mathsf{Aut}_\mathsf{Set}(A)$. The group of permutations of the set $\{1, \dots, n\}$ is denoted by $S_n.$
