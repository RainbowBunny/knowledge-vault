## Definition

> [!definition] Coset
> - The **left-cosets** of a [[Subgroup]] $H$ in a group $G$ are the sets $aH$.
> - The **right-cosets** of $H$ are the sets $Ha, a \in G$.

## Property

> [!proposition]
> There is a one-to-one correspondence between
> - Subgroups of $G$ and [[Equivalence Relation]] on $G$ satisfying 
> $$(\forall g \in G): a \sim b \implies ga \sim gb.$$
> for the relation $\sim_L$ corresponding to a subgroup $H$, $G/_{\sim_L}$ maybe described as the set of left-cosets $aH$ of $H$.
> - Subgroups of $G$ and [[Equivalence Relation]] on $G$ satisfying 
> $$(\forall g \in G): a \sim b \implies ag \sim bg.$$
> for the relation $\sim_R$ corresponding to a subgroup $H$, $G/_{\sim_R}$ maybe described as the set of left-cosets $Ha$ of $H$.

> [!proposition]
> The relation $\sim_L, \sim_R$ corresponding to a [[Subgroup]] $H$ coincide if and only if $H$ is a [[Normal Subgroup]].
