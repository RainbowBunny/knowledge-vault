## Definition

> [!definition] Parameters
> - $p$: a [[Prime]].
> - $G$: a [[Group]].

> [!definition] p-Group
> Extends:: [[Group]].
>
> ---
> A $p$-group is a finite [[Group]] whose order is a power of a prime integer $p$. `[Alu IV §1.1]`

> [!remark] Judson's definition
> [Jud §15.1] calls $G$ a $p$-group if every element of $G$ has as its order a power of $p$, without assuming $G$ finite. For a finite group the two definitions agree: $G$ is a $p$-group if and only if $|G| = p^n$ `[Jud Cor 15.1.2]`.

## Related

### Properties
- [[Group Center]] — a group of order $p^n$ has a nontrivial center `[Jud Thm 14.2.4]`
- [[Group Action]] — for an action of a $p$-group on a finite set $A$ with fixed points $Z$, $|Z| \equiv |A| \bmod p$
