## Definition

> [!definition] Vanishing Polynomial
> Let $\mathbb F$ be a field and $S \subset \mathbb F$. The vanishing polynomial is defined as:
> $$Z_S(X) = \prod_{\alpha \in S}(X - \alpha)$$
> which is a monic polynomial of degree $|S|$.

> [!proposition]
> $$f|_S = 0 \Longleftrightarrow Z_S \;|\; f$$
