## Definition

> [!definition] Parameters
> - $(R, +, \cdot)$: a [[Ring]].
> - $x$: an indeterminate.

> [!definition] Polynomial Ring
> Instantiates:: [[Ring]].
>
> ---
> We define the polynomial ring $(R[x], +, \cdot)$:
> - Elements of the ring are [[Polynomial]] on $R$.
> - Let $f(x) = \sum_{i \geq 0} a_i x^i$ and $g(x) = \sum_{i \geq 0} b_i x^i$:
> 	- $f(x) + g(x) = \sum_{i \geq 0} (a_i + b_i) x^i$.
> 	- $f(x) \cdot g(x) = \sum_{k \geq 0} \sum_{i + j = k} a_i b_j x^{i + j}$.
> - The identity $1_{R[x]} = 1 + 0x + 0x^2 + \cdots$.
>
> `[Alu III.1.3]` `[Jud §17.1]`

## Property

> [!theorem] $\mathbb F[x]$ is a principal ideal domain — every ideal is generated by one polynomial
> If $\mathbb F$ is a [[Field]], then every [[Ideal]] of $\mathbb F[x]$ is principal; since $\mathbb F[x]$ is an [[Integral Domain]], it is a [[Principal Ideal Domain]]. `[Jud Thm 17.3.10]` `[Jud Prop 17.1.4]`
