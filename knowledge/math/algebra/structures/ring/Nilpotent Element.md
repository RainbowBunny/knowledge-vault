## Definition

> [!definition] Nilpotent Element
> An element $a$ of a [[Ring]] $R$ is *nilpotent* if $a^n = 0$ for some $n$.

## Property

> [!proposition]
> If $a$ and $b$ are nilpotent in $R$ and $ab = ba$, then $a + b$ is also nilpotent.
