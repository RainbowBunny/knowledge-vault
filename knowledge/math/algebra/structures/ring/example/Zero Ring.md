## Definition

> [!definition] Zero-ring (Trivial Ring)
> We define the zero-ring $(\{*\}, +, \cdot)$:
> - Elements of the ring is the trivial group $\{*\}$.
> - Operation of the ring:
> 	- $* + * = *$.
> 	- $* \cdot * = *$.

## Property

> [!proposition]
> If there is a [[Ring Homomorphism]] from a zero-ring to a [[Ring]] $R$, then $R$ is a zero-ring.
