## Definition

> [!definition] R-algebra
> Let $R$ be a [[Commutative Ring]]. An $R \mbox{-} \mathsf{algebra}$ is a [[Ring Homomorphism]] $\alpha: R \rightarrow S$ such that $\alpha(R)$ is contained in the [[Ring Center|center]] of $S$.
