## Definition

> [!definition] Left-R-module
> Requires:: [[Ring]], [[Abelian Group]].
> 
> ---
> A left-$R$-module structure on an [[Abelian Group]] $(M, +)$ consists of:
> - $(R, +, \cdot)$: [[Ring]].
> - $(M, +)$: [[Abelian Group]].
> - A map $R \times M \rightarrow M, (r, m) \mapsto r \cdot m$.
> 
> That satisfies [[Distributivity]] for $(+, \cdot)$, [[Associativity]] for $\cdot$, [[Identity Element]] for $\cdot$ (for the map).

> [!definition] Right-R-module
> - The map is now $M \times R \rightarrow M, (m, r) \mapsto m \cdot r$.
