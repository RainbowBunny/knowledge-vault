## Definition

> [!definition] Parameters
> - [[Ring]]: Import $(R, +, *)$.

> [!definition] Division Ring
> Extends:: [[Ring]].
>
> ---
> A **division ring** is a nonzero ring ($1 \neq 0$) in which every nonzero element is a [[Ring#Unit|Two-Sided Unit]].
>
> `[Alu III.1.2]` `[Jud §16.1]` `[WP]`

## Example

> [!example]
> The quaternions $\mathbb H$ form a division ring that is not commutative, hence not a [[Field]]. `[Jud Ex 16.1.7]`
