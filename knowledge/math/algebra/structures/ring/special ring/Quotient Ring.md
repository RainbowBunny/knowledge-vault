## Definition

> [!definition] Quotient Ring
> Let $I$ be an [[Ideal]] of the [[Ring]] $(R, +, \cdot)$. The **quotient ring** of $R$ **modulo** $I$, denoted $R / I$, is defined as the set of [[Coset]] of $I$.
