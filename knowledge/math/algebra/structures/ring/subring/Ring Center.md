## Definition

> [!definition] Center
> Extends:: [[Center]]
> Requires:: [[Ring]], [[Subring]].
> 
> ---
> The **center** of a [[Ring]] $R$ consists of the elements $a$ such that $\forall r \in R: a r = r a$.

## Property

> [!proposition]
> The center is a [[Subring]] of $R$.

> [!proposition]
> The center of a [[Division Ring]] is a [[Field]].
