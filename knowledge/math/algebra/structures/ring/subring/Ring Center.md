## Definition

> [!definition] Parameters
> - $R$: a [[Ring]].

> [!definition] Ring Center
> Extends:: [[Center]].
> Requires:: [[Ring]].
>
> ---
> The **center** of a [[Ring]] $R$, denoted $Z(R)$, consists of the elements $a$ such that $\forall r \in R: a r = r a$.
>
> `[Alu III Ex 2.9]` `[Jud Ex 16.7.32]`

## Property

> [!proposition] Center is a subring — $Z(R)$ is a commutative subring of $R$
> The center is a commutative [[Subring]] of $R$.
>
> `[Alu III Ex 2.9]` `[Jud Ex 16.7.32]`

> [!proposition] Center of a division ring — it is a field
> The center of a [[Division Ring]] is a [[Field]].
>
> `[Alu III Ex 2.9]` `[WP]`
