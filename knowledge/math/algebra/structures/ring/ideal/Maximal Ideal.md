## Definition

> [!definition] Maximal Domain
> Let $I \neq (1)$ be an [[Ideal]] of a [[Commutative Ring]] $R$. $I$ is a **maximal ideal** if $R/I$ is a [[Field]].

## Property

> [!proposition]
> Let $I \neq (1)$ be an [[Ideal]] of a [[Commutative Ring]] $R$:
> - $I$ is maximal if and only if:
> $$I \subseteq J \implies (I = J \lor J = R).$$

> [!proposition]
> Maximal ideal is also a [[Prime Ideal]].
