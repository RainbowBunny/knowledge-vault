## Definition

> [!definition] Parameters
> - $R$: a [[Commutative Ring]].

> [!definition] Noetherian Ring
> Extends:: [[Commutative Ring]].
>
> ---
> A commutative ring $R$ is **Noetherian** if every [[Ideal]] of $R$ is finitely generated.
>
> `[Alu III.4.1]`

> [!remark] Ascending chain condition
> `[Jud §18.2]` defines a Noetherian ring as a commutative ring satisfying the **ascending chain condition**: for every chain of ideals $I_1 \subseteq I_2 \subseteq \cdots$ there is an $N$ with
> $$I_n = I_N \quad \text{for all } n \geq N.$$
> The two definitions are equivalent.
