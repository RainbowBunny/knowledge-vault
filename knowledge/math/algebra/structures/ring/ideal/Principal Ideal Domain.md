## Definition

> [!definition] Principal Ideal Domain
> Extends:: [[Integral Domain]]
> 
> ---
> An integral domain $R$ is a PID ('Principal Ideal Domain') if every [[Ideal]] of $R$ is principal.

> [!example] Example of Principal Ideal Domain
> - $\mathbb{Z}$
