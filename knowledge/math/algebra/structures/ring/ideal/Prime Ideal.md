## Definition

> [!definition] Prime Ideal
> Let $I \neq (1)$ be an [[Ideal]] of a [[Commutative Ring]] $R$. $I$ is a **prime ideal** if $R/I$ is an [[Integral Domain]].

## Property

> [!proposition]
> Let $I \neq (1)$ be an [[Ideal]] of a [[Commutative Ring]] $R$:
> - $I$ is prime if and only if:
> $$\forall a, b \in R: ab \in I \implies (a \in I \lor b \in I).$$

> [!proposition]
> Let $I$ be an [[Ideal]] of a [[Commutative Ring]] $R$. If $R / I$ is finite, then $I$ is prime if and only if it is an [[Maximal Ideal]].

> [!proposition]
> Let $R$ be a [[Principal Ideal Domain]], and let $I$ be a nonzero [[Ideal]] in $R$. Then $I$ is prime if and only if it is maximal.
