## Definition

> [!definition] Parameters
> - $G$: a [[Set]].
> - $\circ$: a [[Binary Operation]] on $G$.

> [!definition] Semigroup
> Extends:: [[Magma]].
>
> ---
> A **semigroup** is a set $G$ together with a binary operation $\circ$ that satisfies [[Closure]], [[Associativity]]. `[AAN]` `[BS81 II §1]`

> [!definition] Semigroup (Alternative)
> Alternatively, a **semigroup** is a [[Magma]] whose binary operation $\circ$ satisfies [[Associativity]]. `[AAN]` `[BS81 II §1]`
