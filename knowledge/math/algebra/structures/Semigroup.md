## Definition

> [!definition] Semigroup
> A **semigroup** is a set $G$ together with a binary operation $\circ$ that satisfies [[Closure]], [[Associativity]].

> [!definition] Semigroup (Alternative)
> Alternatively, a **semigroup** is a [[Magma]] that the binary operation $\circ$ that satisfies [[Associativity]].
