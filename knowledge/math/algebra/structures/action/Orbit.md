## Definition

> [!definition] Orbit
> Requires:: [[Group Action]].
> 
> ---
> The **orbit** of $a \in A$ under an action of a [[Group]] $G$ is the [[Set]]
> $$O_G(a) = \{ga \mid g \in G\}.$$

> [!definition] Trivial Orbit
> The orbit of $a$ is 'trivial' if $|O_G(a)| = 1$.
