## Scope

A [[Magma]] $(M, \star)$ — only [[Commutativity]], checked element by element, is involved.

## Definition

> [!definition] Scope
> A [[Magma]] $(M, \star)$.

> [!definition] Center
> The **center** of $(M, \star)$ is a subset:
> $$Z(M) = \{m \in M \mid (\forall a \in M): a m = m a\}.$$

## Specializations

| where            | what the structure adds                                                                  |
| ---------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| [[Ring Center]]  | the center of $(R, \cdot)$; it is a [[Subring]], and for a [[Division Ring]] a [[Field]] |
| [[Group Center]] | the center of a [[Group]] is an abelian [[Normal Subgroup]]                              |
