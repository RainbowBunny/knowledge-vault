## Definition

> [!definition] Natural Projection
> Let $A, B$ be sets. Then there are **natural projections** $\pi_A, \pi_B$ defined by:
> $$\pi_A((a, b)) = a, \quad \pi_B((a, b)) = b$$
> for all $(a, b) \in A \times B$. Both of these maps are (clearly) surjective.
