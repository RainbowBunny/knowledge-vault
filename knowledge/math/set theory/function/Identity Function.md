## Definition

> [!scheme] Identity Function
> The **identity function** on $A$
> $$\mathrm{id}_A: A \rightarrow A$$
> defined by $$(\forall a \in A) \; \mathrm{id}_A(a) = a.$$

> [!remark]
> If $f: A \rightarrow B$ is any function, then $\mathrm{id}_B \circ f = f$ and $f \circ \mathrm{id}_A = f$. Graphically, the diagrams
> $$\begin{CD} 
> A @>f>> B\\ 
> @| @VV\mathrm{id}_BV\\ 
> A @>f>> B 
> \end{CD}$$
> and
> $$\begin{CD} 
> A @>f>> B\\ 
> @V\mathrm{id}_AVV @|\\ 
> A @>f>> B 
> \end{CD}$$
> commute.

> [!remark]
> The identity function $\mathrm{id}_A: A \rightarrow A$ is a [[Bijection]].
