## Definition

> [!definition] Linearity
> ### Scope
> A map $f: A \rightarrow B$
> 
> ---
> ### Condition
> - $A, B$ are [[Vector Spaces]] over the same [[Field]] $\mathbb{F}$.
> 
> ---
> ### Property
> $f$ is linear iff for all $x, y \in A$ and $\alpha \in F$:
> - $f(x + y) = f(x) + f(y)$ (Preserves vector addition).
> - $f(\alpha x) = \alpha f(x)$ (Preserves scalar multiplication).
