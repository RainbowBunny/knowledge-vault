## Definition

> [!definition] Congruence
> Let $n$ be a positive integer. Consider the [[Equivalence Relation]] on $\mathbb{Z}$ defined by
> $$(\forall a, b \in \mathbb{Z}): a \equiv b \mod n \iff n \mid (b - a).$$
> This is called **congruence module** $n$.

> [!remark]
> The set of equivalence classes is often denoted by $\mathbb{Z}_n, \mathbb{Z}/(n), \mathbb{Z}/n\mathbb{Z}$, or $\mathbb{F}_n$. The most common one is $\mathbb{Z}_n$.

> [!lemma]
> If $a \equiv a' \mod n$ and $b \equiv b' \mod n$, then
> $$(a + a') \equiv (b + b') \mod n$$
> and
> $$a a' \equiv b b' \mod n$$
