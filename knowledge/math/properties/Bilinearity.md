## Basic Definition

> [!definition] Bilinear
> ### Scope
> A map $f: M \times N \rightarrow P$
> 
> ---
> ### Condition
> - $M, N, P$ is a modules over a commutative ring $R$ (or vector spaces over a field).
> 
> ---
> ### Property
> $f$ is bilinear iff it is linear in each argument with the other held fixed:
> - $f(x + x', y) = f(x, y) + f(x', y)$
> - $f(x, y + y') = f(x, y) + f(x, y')$
> - $f(rx, y) = rf(x, y) = f(x, ry)$ for $r \in R$
