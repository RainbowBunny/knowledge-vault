## Definition

> [!definition] Bilinear
> ### Scope
> A map $f: V_1 \times \cdots \times V_k \rightarrow W$
> 
> ---
> ### Condition
> - $V_1, \cdots, V_k, W$ is a modules over a commutative ring $R$ (or vector spaces over a field).
> 
> ---
> ### Property
> $f$ is multilinear iff it is [[Linearity|Linear]] in each argument with the other held fixed.
