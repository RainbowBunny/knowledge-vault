## Basic Definition

> [!definition] Bijective
> ### Scope
> A [[Function]] $f: A \rightarrow B$
> 
> ---
> ### Property
> $f$ is **bijective** if $f$ is both an [[Injection]] and a [[Surjection]],
> We denoted as $f: A \stackrel{\sim}{\rightarrow} B$ and also write $A \stackrel{\sim}{=} B$. We say that $A$ and $B$ are 'isomorphic' sets.
