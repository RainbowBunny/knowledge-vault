## Basic Definition

> [!definition] Alternativity
> ### Scope
> A [[Binary Operation]] $\star$.
> 
> ---
> ### Property
> An operation $\star$ is alternative iff $\forall a, b \in O$:
> - $a \star (a \star b) = (a \star a) \star b$ (Left alternative).
> - $(a \star b) \star b = a \star (b \star b)$ (Right alternative).
