## Basic Definition

> [!definition] Absorption
> ### Scope
> Two [[Binary Operation]] $\oplus: O \times O \rightarrow O, \otimes: O \times O \rightarrow O$.
> 
> ---
> ### Property
> Two binary operations $\oplus, \otimes$ satisfy **absorption** iff for all $a, b \in O$:
> - $a \oplus (a \otimes a) = a$.
> - $a \otimes (a \oplus a) = a$.
