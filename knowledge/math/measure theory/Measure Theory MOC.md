# Measure Theory MOC

Measures on σ-algebras, and the measures a random dynamical system leaves unchanged.

## Theory

- [[Sigma Algebra]] — the sets a measure is defined on; the Borel σ-algebra of a topological space
- [[Measure]] — countable additivity; probability measures; the push-forward $f_*\mu$ and the change of variables
- [[Stationary Measure]] — one random step leaves the law unchanged; Kakutani's theorem (on a compact space one exists), the Choquet–Deny theorem (for abelian semigroups stationary means invariant); the Cantor example

## Dynamics

- [[Iterated Function System]] — finitely many contractions; Hutchinson's theorem (exactly one compact invariant set, the attractor) and the invariant measure supported on it

## Related

- Prerequisites: [[Topological Space]], [[Metric Space]], [[Contraction]], [[Semigroup]]
- [[Probability MOC]] — the finite probability spaces there are measures of total mass $1$ on a finite set
