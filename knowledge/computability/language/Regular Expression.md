## Definition

> [!definition] Regular Expression
> Define the **Regular Expression** $\mathcal{R}(\Sigma)$ with $\Sigma$ is an alphabet as follows:
> An expression $R$ is a Regular Expression if $R$ is:
> 1. $a$ for some $a$ in $\Sigma$.
> 2. $\varepsilon$.
> 3. $\emptyset$.
> 4. $R_1 \cup R_2$ for some regular expression $R_1, R_2$.
> 5. $R_1 \circ R_2$ for some regular expression $R_1, R_2$.
> 6. $R_1^{*}$ for a regular expression $R_1$.
