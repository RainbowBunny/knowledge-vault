## Formulation

> [!definition] Arithmetic Circuit Satisfiability Problem
> The arithmetic $\mathcal C$ takes two inputs:
> - $x$: Public and fixed input (known to both the prover and verifier).
> - $w$: Witness (non-deterministic input or auxiliary input).
> Given the first input $x$ and output $y$, the goal is to determine whether there exists a $w$ such that $\mathcal C(x, w) = y$.
