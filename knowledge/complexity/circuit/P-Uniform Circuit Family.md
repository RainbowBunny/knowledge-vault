## Definition

> [!definition] P-Uniform Circuit Family
> Generalizes:: [[Circuit Family]]
> 
> ---
> A circuit family $\{C_n\}$ is $\mathsf{P}$-uniform if there is a polynomial-time [[Offline Turing Machine|Turing Machine]] that on input $1^n$ outputs the description of the circuit $C_n$.
