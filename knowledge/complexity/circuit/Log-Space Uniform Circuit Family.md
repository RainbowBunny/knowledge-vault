## Definition

> [!definition] Log-Space Uniform Circuit Family
> Generalizes:: [[Circuit Family]]
> 
> ---
> A circuit family $\{C_n\}$ is log-space uniform if there is an [[Computable Function|Implicitly Log-Space Computable Function]] mapping $1^n$ to the description of the circuit $C_n$.

> [!definition] Log-Space Uniform Circuit Family (Alternative)
> Alternatively, the circuit representing function $\mathsf{SIZE}, \mathsf{TYPE}, \mathsf{EDGE}$ are computable in $O(\log n)$ space.
