## Definition

> [!definition] Quantified Boolean Formula
> A **quantified Boolean formula** (QBF) in the **prenex normal form** is a formula of the form
> $$Q_1 x_1 \cdots Q_n x_n \varphi(x_1, \dots, x_n)$$
> where:
> - $Q_i$ is one of the two quantifiers $\forall$ or $\exists$.
> - $x_1, \dots, x_n \in \{0, 1\}$.
> - $\varphi$: A plain (unquantified) Boolean formula.
