## Definition

> [!definition] Qubit
> The state of qubit at any time is called a **superposition** of these basic states.
> - The basic states by $|0 \rangle$ and $|1 \rangle$.
> - A qubit is any state $\alpha_0 | 0 \rangle + \alpha_1 | 1 \rangle$, where $\alpha_0, \alpha_1$ are called **amplitudes** and are complex numbers satisfying $|\alpha_0|^2 + |\alpha_1|^2 = 1$.
> - When the qubit is observed, with probability $|\alpha_0|^2$, it is revealed to be the state zero ($|0 \rangle$) and with probability $|\alpha_1|^2$ it is revealed to be in state one ($|1 \rangle$). After observation the amplitude wave **collapses**, and the values of the amplitudes are irretrievably lost.
