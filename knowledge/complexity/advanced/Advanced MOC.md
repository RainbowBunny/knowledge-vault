# Advanced MOC

Specialized branches of complexity theory. Each is its own subfield with its own conferences and surveys; stubbed here for future expansion. Once any of these grows substantial content, it should be promoted to its own first-level folder under `complexity/`.

- [[Approximation Hardness]] — PCP theorem, inapproximability, optimal approximation ratios
- [[Communication Complexity]] — two-party (and multi-party) computation with limited communication; Yao's framework
- [[Parameterized Complexity]] — FPT, W-hierarchy, kernelization
- [[Average-Case Complexity]] — distNP, Levin's theory, hardness amplification
- [[Fine-Grained Complexity]] — SETH-conditional lower bounds; APSP, OV, edit-distance
