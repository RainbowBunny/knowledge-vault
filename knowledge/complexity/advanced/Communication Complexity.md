# Communication Complexity

How many bits must two (or more) parties exchange to jointly compute a function of their private inputs? Yao's framework (1979).

Stub. Topics to cover:

- **Two-party communication** — Alice with $x$, Bob with $y$, compute $f(x, y)$. Minimum bits over the worst case.
- **Deterministic vs. randomized vs. quantum** — $D(f), R(f), Q(f)$.
- **Lower bound techniques** — fooling sets, rank lower bound, discrepancy, log-rank conjecture.
- **Communication complexity classes** — analogs of P, NP, BPP in the communication setting.
- **Applications** — circuit lower bounds (Karchmer-Wigderson), streaming lower bounds, data-structure lower bounds.
