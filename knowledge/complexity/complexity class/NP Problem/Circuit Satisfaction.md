## Scheme

> [!definition] Circuit Satisfaction
> The circuit satisfaction problem of a circuit $C: I_u \times I_w \rightarrow \{0, 1\}$ is defined by the relation $\mathcal R_C = \{(u, w) \in I_u \times I_w : \mathcal C(u, w) = 1\}$ and its language is $\mathcal L_C = \{u \in I_u: \exists w \in I_w, C(u, w) = 1\}$.
