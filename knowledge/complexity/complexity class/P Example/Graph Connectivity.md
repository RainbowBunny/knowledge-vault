## Definition

> [!definition] Graph Connectivity
> Input:
> - $G$: Graph.
> - $s, t$: Two vertices in the graph.
> 
> ---
> Output: Decide if s is connected to t in G.
> 
> ---
> Certificate: A path from $s$ to $t$.
