## Definition

> [!definition] $\# \mathsf{SAT}_D$
> $\# \mathsf{SAT}_D = \{\langle \phi, K \rangle: \phi \text{ is a 3CNF formula and it has exactly } K \text{ satisfying assignments}\}$
