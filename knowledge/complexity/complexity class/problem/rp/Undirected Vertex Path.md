## Definition

> [!definition] Undirected Vertex Path
> Reference Name: $\mathsf{UPATH}$
> 
> ---
> Input:
> - $G$: Undirected graph.
> - $s, t$: Two vertices in the graph.
> 
> ---
> Output: Decide if s is connected to t in G.

## Property

> [!theorem]
> Requires:: [[Class RL]]
> 
> ---
> $\mathsf{UPATH} \in \mathsf{RL}$.
