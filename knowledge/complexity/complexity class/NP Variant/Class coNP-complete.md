## Definition

> [!definition] Class coNP-complete
> Requires:: [[Polynomial-time Karp Reducibility]]
> 
> ---
> A [[Language]] $\mathcal{L} \in \mathsf{coNP}\mbox{-}\mathsf{complete}$ if:
> $$\mathcal{L} \in \mathsf{coNP} \land \forall \mathcal{L'} \in \mathsf{coNP}: \mathcal{L} \leq_p \mathcal{L}'$$
