## Property

> [!theorem] Ladner's Theorem
> Requires:: [[Class P]], [[Class NP]], [[Hardness and Completeness|NP-complete]]
> 
> ---
> Suppose that $\mathsf{P} \neq \mathsf{NP}$. Then there exists a [[Language]] $\mathcal{L} \in \mathsf{NP} \backslash \mathsf{P}$ that is not $\mathsf{NP}\mbox{-}\mathsf{complete}$.
