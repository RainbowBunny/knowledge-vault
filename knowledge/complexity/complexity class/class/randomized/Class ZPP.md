## Definition

> [!definition] Zero-error Probabilistic Polynomial time
> Requires:: [[Class ZTIME]]
> 
> ---
> $\mathsf{ZPP} = \cup_{c > 0} \mathsf{ZTIME}(n^c)$.

## Property

### Relation to Other Classes

> [!theorem]
> Requires:: [[Class RP]], [[Class coRP]]
> 
> ---
> $\mathsf{RPP} = \mathsf{RP} \cap \mathsf{coRP}$.
