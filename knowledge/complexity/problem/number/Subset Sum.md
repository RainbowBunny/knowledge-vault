## Definition

> [!definition] Subset Sum
> Input:
> - $n$: Number of elements.
> - $A_1, \dots, A_n$: A list of $n$ elements.
> - $T$: The target sum.
> 
> ---
> Output: Decide if there is a subset of the numbers that sums up to $T$.
> 
> ---
> Certificate: List of members in such a subset.

## Property

> [!theorem]
> Requires:: [[Hardness and Completeness]]
> Complete for:: [[Class NP]] under [[Polynomial-time Karp Reducibility]]
