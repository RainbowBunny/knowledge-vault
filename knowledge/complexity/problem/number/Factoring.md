## Definition

> [!definition] Factoring
> Input:
> - $N$: A number. 
> - $L$: Lower bound of the range.
> - $U$: Upper bound of the range.
> 
> ---
> Output: Decide if $N$ has a prime factor $p$ in the interval $[L, U]$.
> 
> ---
> Certificate: The factor $p$.

## Property

> [!proposition]
> Requires:: [[Hardness and Completeness]]
> Member of:: [[Class NP]]
