## Definition

> [!definition] Distributional Problem
> A **distributional problem** is a pair $\langle \mathcal{L}, \mathcal{D} \rangle$ where $\mathcal{L} \subseteq \{0, 1\}^*$ is a [[Language]], and $\mathcal{D} = \{\mathcal{D}_n\}$ is a sequence of distribution, with $\mathcal{D}_n$ being a distribution over $\{0, 1\}^n.$
