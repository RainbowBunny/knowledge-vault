## Basic Definition

> [!definition] Effective Relation
> An **effective relation** is a binary relation $\mathcal R \subset \mathcal X \times \mathcal W$, where $\mathcal X, \mathcal W$ and $\mathcal R$ are efficiently recognizable finite sets. Elements of $\mathcal X$ are called **statements**. If $(x, w) \in \mathcal R$, then $w$ is called a **witness for** $x$.
