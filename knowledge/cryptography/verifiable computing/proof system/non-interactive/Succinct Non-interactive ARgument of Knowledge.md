## Definition

> [!definition] Succinct Non-interactive ARgument of Knowledge
> Extends:: [[Non-interactive ARgument of Knowledge]]
>
> ---
> A **succinct non-interactive argument of knowledge** (SNARK) is a [[Non-interactive ARgument of Knowledge|NARK]] with [[Succinctness]].

> [!remark] Also a SNARG
> `⚠️ CLAUDE – verify:` a SNARK is also a [[Succinct Non-interactive ARGument|SNARG]], because knowledge soundness implies soundness. That is the usual reading, but it depends on how adaptivity and auxiliary input are set in the two definitions; check [[Knowledge Soundness]] against [[Soundness]] before relying on it.
