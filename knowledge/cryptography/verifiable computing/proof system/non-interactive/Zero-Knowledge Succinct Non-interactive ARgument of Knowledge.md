## Definition

> [!definition] Zero-Knowledge Succinct Non-interactive ARgument of Knowledge
> Extends:: [[Succinct Non-interactive ARgument of Knowledge|SNARK]]
>
> ---
> A **zero-knowledge SNARK** is a [[Succinct Non-interactive ARgument of Knowledge|SNARK]] that is also [[Zero Knowledge]]. In terms of the base syntax: a [[Non-Interactive Proof Systems]] that is complete, knowledge sound, succinct and zero-knowledge.
