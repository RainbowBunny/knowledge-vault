## Scheme

> [!definition] Parameters

> [!scheme] Alekhnovich Encryption Scheme
> Instantiates:: [[Public-Key Encryption Scheme]].
> 
> ---
> - $\mathsf{Gen}(1^\lambda)$:
> 	1. Samples $\mathbf{H} \leftarrow \mathbb{F}_q^{(n - k - 1) \times n}$.
> 	2. Samples $\mathbf{e} \leftarrow \mathcal{B}(\sigma)^{\otimes n}$.
> 	3. Generates generator matrix $\mathbf{G} \in \mathbb{F}_q^{k \times n}$ of the parity-check matrix of the code generated by $(\mathbf{H} || \mathbf{e})$.
> 	4. Outputs $(\mathrm{pk} = \mathbf{G}, \mathrm{sk} = \mathbf{e})$.
> - $\mathsf{Enc}(\mathrm{pk}, m) \rightarrow \mathrm{ct}$:
> 	1. If $m = 0$:
> 		1. Samples $\mathbf{x} \leftarrow \mathbb{F}_q^k$.
> 		2. Samples $\mathbf{e}' \leftarrow \mathcal{B}(\omega)^{\otimes n}$.
> 		3. Outputs $\mathrm{ct} = \mathbf{x} \mathbf{G} + \mathbf{e}'$.
> 	2. If $m = 1$:
> 		1. Outputs $\mathrm{ct} \leftarrow \mathbb{F}_q^n$.
> - $\mathsf{Dec}(\mathrm{sk}, \mathrm{ct}) \rightarrow m$:
> 	1. Outputs $m = [\mathbf{e} \cdot \mathrm{ct}^\top \neq 0]$.

> [!scheme] Alekhnovich Encryption Scheme (Multi-Receiver Variant)
> Instantiates:: [[Multi-Receiver Encryption Scheme]]
> 
> ---
> - $\mathsf{Gen}(1^\lambda, N)$:
> 	- Now we generates $(\mathbf{e}_i)_{i \in [N]}$ instead of $\mathbf{e}$, and thus $\mathbf{H} \leftarrow \mathbb{F}_q^{(n - k - N) \times n}$.
> 	- Also, $\mathrm{sk}_i = \mathbf{e}_i$.
> - $\mathsf{Enc}(\mathrm{pk}, m)$: Unchanged.
> - $\mathsf{Dec}(\mathrm{sk}_i, m)$: Unchanged.
