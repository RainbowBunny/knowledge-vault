## Problem

> [!definition] Shortest Basis Problem ($\text{SBP}$)
> Find a basis $\textbf{v}_1, \dots, \textbf{v}_n$ for a lattice that is shortest in some sense. For example, we might require that $$\max_{1 \leq i \leq n} ||\textbf{v}_i|| \quad \text{or} \quad \sum_{i = 1}^n ||\textbf{v}_i||^2$$ be minimized. There are thus many different versions of $\text{SBP}$, depending on how one decides to measure the "size" of a basis.
