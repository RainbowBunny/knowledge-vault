## Assumption

> [!conjecture] Discrete-Logarithm Assumption
> Given a cyclic group $\mathbb G$ of order $n \in \mathbb N$ with a generator $g$, the discrete logarithm assumption over $\mathbb G$ states, that it is computationally infeasible given a random group element $h \in \mathbb G$ to find an integer $x \in \mathbb Z_n$ such that $h = g^x$.
