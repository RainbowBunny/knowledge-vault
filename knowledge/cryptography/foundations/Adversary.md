## Basic Definition

> [!definition] Adversary
> An adversary is **an algorithm**, subject to resource constraints, that plays a game against a challenger, and security is the claim that no algorithm in the admissible class wins that game with noticeably probability better than trivial guessing.

> [!definition] Advantage
> We refer to the different between the adversary and trivial guessing as **advantage**.

### Computational

> [!definition] Computational
> Only require that the advantage is negligible for computationally bounded adversary.

### Statistical

> [!definition] Statistical
> The adversary is unbounded but the advantage is still negligible.

### Perfect

> [!definition] Perfect
> The advantage is $0$.
