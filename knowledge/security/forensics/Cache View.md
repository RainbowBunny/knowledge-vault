## Chrome, Discord

Tool: [Chrome Cache View](https://www.nirsoft.net/utils/chrome_cache_view.html)

Path to look for: 
- `AppData/Roaming/discord/Cache/Cache_Data`
- `AppData/Local/Google/Chrome/User Data/Default/Cache`

Command: `ChromeCacheView.exe -folder`
