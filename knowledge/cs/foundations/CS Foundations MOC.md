# Foundations MOC

Meta-level material on what algorithms are, how to reason about them, and the formal language they operate on.

- [[Algorithm]] — definition, pseudocode conventions, loop invariants, analysis & design paradigms (incremental, divide-and-conquer)
- [[Asymptotic Analysis]] — worst-case / average-case, Big-O / Ω / Θ, negligible & poly-bounded functions
- [[Solving Recurrences]] — substitution method, recursion-tree method, master theorem
- [[Languages]] — alphabets, symbols, strings, languages
- [[Computability Theory]] — Turing machines, decidability, undecidability, reducibility, recursion theorem
