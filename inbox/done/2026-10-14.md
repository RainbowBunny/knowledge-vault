### Targeted Malleability: Homomorphic Encryption for Restricted Computations

https://eprint.iacr.org/2011/311.pdf

> [!definition] semantically secure against Chosen Plaintext Attack
> CPA Experiment $\mathsf{Expt}^{\mathsf{CPA}}_{\Pi, b}(\lambda, \mathcal{A} = (\mathcal{A}_\mathsf{find}, \mathcal{A}_\mathsf{guess}))$: 
> 1. $(\mathrm{pk}, \mathrm{sk}) \leftarrow \mathsf{Gen}(1^\lambda)$.
> 2. $(m_0, m_1) \leftarrow \mathcal{A}_\mathsf{find}(1^\lambda, \mathrm{pk})$ such that $|m_0| = |m_1|$.
> 3. $c^* \leftarrow \mathsf{Enc}(\mathrm{pk}, m_b)$.
> 4. $b' \leftarrow \mathcal{A}_\mathrm{guess}(c^*)$.
> 
> Adversary's win condition: $b' = 1$.
> 
> ---
> 
> A [[Public-Key Encryption Scheme]] is semantically secure against chosen-plaintext attacks if for any [[PPT]] adversary $\mathcal{A}$.
> $$\mathsf{Adv}^{\mathsf{CPA}}_{\Pi}(\mathcal{A}) = |\Pr[\mathsf{Expt}_{\Pi, 0}^{\mathsf{CPA}}(\lambda, \mathcal{A})] - \Pr[\mathsf{Expt}_{\Pi, 1}^{\mathsf{CPA}}(\lambda, \mathcal{A})] |$$
