## The Elasticity of Demand

### The Price Elasticity of Demand and Its Determinants

4 determinants:
- **Availability of Close Substitutes:** A good with close substitutes tends to have more elastic demand because it is easier for consumers to switch from that good to others. 
- **Necessities versus Luxuries:** Necessities tend to have inelastic demands, whereas luxuries have elastic demands. Note that whether a good is a necessity or a luxury depends not on the good's intrinsic properties but on the buyer's preferences.
