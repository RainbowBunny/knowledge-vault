## Homophonic Cipher

> [!definition] Homophonic Cipher
> A **homophonic cipher** is a substitution cipher in which there may be more than one ciphertext symbol for each plaintext letter.

## Transposition Cipher

> [!definition] Transposition Cipher
> A **transposition cipher** is a cipher in which the letters of the plaintext remain the same, but their order is rearranged.
