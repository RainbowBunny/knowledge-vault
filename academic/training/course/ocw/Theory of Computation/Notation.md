$\mathcal P$: Powerset (Set of all subset)
1. $R$ is reflexive if for every $x$, $xRx$; 
2. $R$ is symmetric if for every $x$ and $y$, $xRy$ implies $yRx$; and 
3. $R$ is transitive if for every $x$, $y$, and $z$, $xRy$ and $yRz$ implies $xRz$.
